Task Instructions

On each trial, you will see two 3D tetrahedral molecules side by side. Both molecules share the exact same chemical formula and connectivity, but differ in their spatial orientation or configuration.

Your goal is to mentally rotate the molecules and decide within 5.0 seconds whether they represent the same compound (Identical) or non-superimposable mirror images (Stereoisomers / Enantiomers).
